- **More reliable transitions between AirPlay and other casting sources.** An ended AirPlay session no longer clears an active desktop or DLNA session, and the music screen returns when an iPhone resumes audio after an AirPlay video ends.
- **Keep the current screen when takeover is disabled.** Checking a new AirPlay connection no longer replaces the active cast with a false connecting state when other devices are not allowed to take over.
