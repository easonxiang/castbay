- **Find the casting controls more easily.** The waiting screen shows how to open the quick menu with the remote. Changing the picture mode briefly shows the selected mode.
- **More responsive TV settings.** Reduced the work done when navigating settings and fixed a pause when receiver settings require a restart.
- **Control whether CastBay starts at boot.** The new setting is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings → General.
- **More reliable DLNA shutdown.** Incoming requests are handled safely while the receiver stops.
