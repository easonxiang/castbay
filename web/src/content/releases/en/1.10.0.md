- **Traditional Chinese.** Settings, under General, now offers 繁體中文 next to 简体中文. Many thanks to KINO Studio for the translation. Anyone who had chosen the Chinese option keeps Simplified Chinese.
- **Video links no longer run low-memory devices out of memory.** Casting a video link (HLS) to a car unit or another device with little memory could fill it up and close the app. The buffer is now kept smaller on such devices; TVs with plenty of memory keep the buffer they had. The problem reports also say how much memory the device has.
- **A car whose clock jumps at start-up no longer crashes CastBay on Android 8 and 9.** The receiver stopped itself after a failure while another start was waiting, and those Android versions then closed the app.
- **The receiver stays running while it retries.** After a failure CastBay starts the receiver again by itself; before, that ran without the notification and the system could end it at any time. The notification now says it is retrying.
- **Lyrics:** instrumental tracks are recognised and say so instead of searching for lyrics, and a busy LRCLIB is retried instead of ending the search.
- Smaller: the names the system shows for an untitled track and untitled AirPlay audio are translated.
