## Who should pick CastBay

- You want to cast from an iPhone, iPad or Mac to an Android TV and you are tired of ads, subscriptions or a session timer cutting the video off.
- You want a receiver that works without an account and does not send viewing data anywhere.
- You are fine using AirPlay or DLNA. If you need Google Cast (Chromecast) or Miracast, CastBay is not the right receiver yet — see the FAQ.

## Who should pick something else

- You cast mainly from Android phones with apps that only offer Google Cast (for example YouTube for Android). CastBay does not support Google Cast; use the TV's own app or an AirPlay/DLNA-capable receiver instead.
- You need Miracast. CastBay does not support it.
